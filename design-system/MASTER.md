## Typography (Developer Mono)

- **Mono (labels, numbers, prompt):** JetBrains Mono (subset + base64 in SVG)
- **Sans (body):** IBM Plex Sans (subset + base64 in SVG)
- **Fallback:** `ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace` / `system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Do not** rely on external `@import` fonts in README images — embed subset woff2.

Label style: monospace, accent or muted, e.g. `// impact`, `~/anhquoc $ whoami`.
